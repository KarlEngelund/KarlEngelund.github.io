About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Copenhagen, where I work in the Insurance and Economics section under the supervision of Jesper Lund Pedersen and Thomas Mikosch. I have also been a visiting scholar at Columbia University, hosted by Richard Davis and Steven Campbell, and at the University of Technology Sydney, hosted by Kristoffer Glover.

My research is motivated by problems in mathematical finance, stochastic control, and decision-making under uncertainty. I am particularly interested in optimal stopping problems, stochastic games, and sequential statistical inference. My work combines probabilistic methods, stochastic analysis, and numerical techniques to study decision problems involving incomplete information, learning, and strategic interactions.
